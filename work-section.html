The work

Concept work,
with the thinking shown.

Three concepts, from the insight to the executions. Each one shows what we noticed about the audience, the bet we would put in market, the variable the executions hold under test, and the decision the result would drive. Concept projects, built to show method. Not client campaigns, and no performance claims.

Project 01

Order the coffee.Subscription language-learning app

The setup
A language app selling a subscription. Like most of the category, its ads sell the streak: days in a row, badges, a bar that fills up.
What we noticed
Nobody installs a language app to build a streak. They install it with a moment in mind: a trip, a partner's parents, a job interview. The ads sell the mechanic and leave the moment unsaid. Cancellations say the same thing from the other end, that people leave still unable to do anything real.
The bet
We would test whether naming the moment someone installed for moves a cold audience more than the habit that is supposed to get them there.
Why it could work

Streaks measure effort. A moment measures arrival. The whole category sells the climb, so the one brand showing the summit looks different by default. The constraint is that the timeframe has to be one the product can keep: an acquisition promise the onboarding cannot deliver only buys a cancellation six weeks later.

What varies

The moment, and the timeframe attached to it. Ordering a coffee, reading a menu, getting through a conversation with a partner's parents, each with and without a stated number of weeks. Format and product framing are held steady so the moment is the only thing under test.

What we would learn next

If a moment separates, the next drop varies the timeframe against it to find the shortest claim people believe and the product can keep. If nothing beats the streak control, the insight is wrong and we return to research rather than producing more of the same idea.

Project 02

Everyone leaves with the same record.Prosumer AI assistant

The setup
A subscription assistant that turns a meeting into one shared written record. The category sells the technology: AI-powered, automatic, real-time.
What we noticed
The failure is not that nobody took notes. It is that four people left with four versions of what was agreed, and nobody finds out until the work comes back wrong.
The bet
We would test whether showing the artefact itself, the shared record and the mechanism that produces it, sells harder than describing the capability behind it.
Why it could work

This is a mechanism claim, not a mood. It can be demonstrated on screen in a few seconds, a buyer can check it, and someone comparing three similar tools can act on it. Product-led proof also survives the sceptical viewer who discounts an emotional appeal outright.

What varies

The proof type. The artefact on its own, the mechanism that produces it, a side-by-side of four recollections against one record, and a timed demonstration. The claim is held constant while the evidence for it changes.

What we would learn next

If the artefact alone carries it, the product can lead every execution and the pipeline goes static-first, which is cheaper and faster to iterate. If the mechanism is the part that convinces, motion becomes the primary format and the next drop tests how much process to show before the payoff.

Project 03

Built for people who have quit before.Digital education platform

The setup
A subscription platform teaching a professional skill to people outside the profession. Deliberately not an app, to show the method travels.
What we noticed
Price is not the objection and neither is the syllabus. The buyer has bought a course before, not finished it, and does not want to pay to feel that again.
The bet
We would test whether naming the fear of not finishing does more work than selling the outcome that only arrives after finishing.
Why it could work

Every competitor advertises the destination. This takes the buyer's real objection off the table before they have to raise it, which is cheaper than arguing with it on the landing page.

What varies

The reassurance offered against the objection. A visible path, an hours-per-week number, a peer who had also quit, or nothing at all. The objection is held constant while the answer to it changes.

What we would learn next

If the bare objection wins the click but not the signup, the angle is right and the landing page is the problem, which is a cheaper fix than new creative. If one reassurance separates, it becomes the product's headline claim and the next drop tests how early it can appear.
