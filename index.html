You don't need more versions of the same ad. You need new ideas to test. When a winning ad starts to fatigue, changing the headline, colour or layout doesn't necessarily create a new idea. More creative ideas worth testing. Three concepts, from the insight to the ads. Each summary shows what we noticed about the audience, the bet we would put in market, and the decision the result would drive. Concept projects, built to show method. Not client campaigns, and no performance claims.

01 Subscription language-learning app Concept summary

Order the coffee.

Nobody installs a language app to build a streak. They install it with a moment in mind, and the category's ads never say so.

The bet

We would test whether naming the moment someone installed for moves a cold audience more than the habit that is supposed to get them there.

What the test would teach us: Whether a moment separates from the streak control, and if one does, which timeframe people believe and the product can actually keep.

02 Prosumer AI assistant Concept summary

Everyone leaves with the same notes.

The failure is not that nobody took notes. It is that four people left with four versions of what was agreed.

The bet

We would test whether showing the artefact itself, the shared notes and the mechanism that produces them, sells harder than describing the capability behind it.

What the test would teach us: If the artefact alone carries it, the product can lead every ad and the pipeline goes static-first. If the mechanism is what convinces, motion becomes the primary format for the next drop.

03 Online learning platform Concept summary

From first lesson to career move.

Professionals are not short of content. They are short of leverage, and the advertising never mentions the deadline driving them.

The bet

We would test whether leading with the career outcome someone is working toward does more than leading with the size of the catalogue.

What the test would teach us: If one outcome frame separates, it becomes the headline claim and the next drop tests how specific the timeframe can be before it stops being believed.

Questions

Before you ask.

Who is Kalvano for?

Subscription apps and digital-first brands already investing in paid acquisition. Selected financial and trading brands can also be a fit where the work matches the performance-creative offer.

Do you run the ads or manage social accounts?

No. We are specialists in creative strategy, production and iteration. Your media buying team, in house or external, runs the campaigns and we build from their results.

What is included in a Creative Sprint?

Three strategic ad concepts, 18 ready-to-test ads (primarily static creative), a recommended testing structure, a 7-day turnaround and one revision round. Motion or video is not guaranteed within the standard Sprint and can be scoped separately.

Why do several creatives come from one concept?

One advertising idea should not be judged from one execution. We develop meaningful versions through different hooks, visuals, formats, opening frames and messaging, so your team can test the idea properly and iterate what works.

What do you need from us to start?

Access to your current creative, whatever performance data you can share, and one kickoff call. If there is a brand guideline or existing footage, we will use it.

Can you use our existing UGC or video footage?

Yes. If you already have creator footage or product video we will build with it. We do not currently source creators or run shoots.

What formats do you produce?

Primarily static paid-social ads. Motion is available selectively when it materially improves the test, and video is scoped separately rather than included by default. If you already have UGC or footage, we can repurpose it into ads. The format follows the advertising hypothesis, not the other way around.

What happens if a concept does not perform?

That is information, and it is why we put more than one idea into the market at a time. A concept that loses still tells you something about the audience, and that shapes the next round. On a Sprint you keep everything produced. On a Creative Engine, the losing idea is retired and the next drop tests a different one.

Who will we actually be working with?

You work directly with the person doing the research, the strategy and the creative direction. Specialist production is brought in where a concept calls for it. There is no account layer and no handover to a separate team.

How does an engagement start?

Most engagements start with a Creative Sprint. The first work puts new concepts into your testing pipeline, then the results decide whether an ongoing Creative Engine makes sense.
