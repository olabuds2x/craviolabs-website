Is Kalvano a fit?

A good fit if…

  • You’re already running paid ads and have results to test against.
  • Your current creative feels exhausted or all sounds the same.
  • You have a media buyer or team to run the campaigns.

Not a fit if…

  • You need video or motion as the core deliverable.
  • You’re pre-launch, with no customer feedback or market evidence to work from.
  • You want someone to run the ads as well as make them.
Questions

Before you ask.

What do you need from us to start?

Product information, brand assets and current ads, plus a kickoff call and one designated approver. Share any available customer feedback and campaign data. You do not need a detailed creative brief or direct access to customers. We confirm receipt of payment and required inputs before the seven-day Sprint starts.

Do you run the ads or guarantee results?

No. We handle the research, concepts, copy and static creative. Your team or media buyer handles campaign setup, media buying and optimisation. Results also depend on your offer, landing page and campaign execution. Available results inform future work on the Engine, including its monthly creative review and next-month testing plan.

What counts as a concept, version or asset?

A concept is a distinct advertising idea. A version explores it through a different hook, message or visual. An asset is one finished export: one version in three sizes counts as three assets. We agree the mix before production. The Sprint includes 3 concepts and 18 static assets; the Engine includes 4 new concepts and up to 30 static assets across two agreed monthly drops.

How do revisions work?

The Sprint includes one consolidated revision round; the Engine includes one per drop. Your approver sends one collected set of feedback on the agreed direction. New concepts, changed briefs or extra rounds require agreed additional scope or a future Engine drop. Corrections to our own mistakes do not use your revision allowance.

How do billing and cancellation work?

The US$1,500 Sprint is a one-time engagement for new clients, paid upfront with no ongoing commitment. Subsequent standalone projects are quoted separately. The US$2,500/month Engine is billed in advance with no minimum multi-month commitment. Cancel before your next billing date to stop renewal; we complete the current paid month’s agreed work. Continue from a Sprint to the Engine within 30 days and your US$1,500 Sprint fee counts toward the first month: US$1,000 remaining for that month, then US$2,500/month.

Do you create video ads too?

Our standard packages focus on static paid-social creative. Motion, video editing and repurposing supplied UGC or product footage can be scoped and priced separately. Creator sourcing and shoots are not included.
