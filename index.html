What it costs you

    You keep paying to test a message you’ve already tested.

    If your ads say what your competitors say, customers have little reason to choose you.

Questions

Before you ask.

Still unsure? Book a 20-minute intro (opens in a new tab) and ask us directly.

What do you need from us to start?

Product information, brand assets and current ads, plus a kickoff call and one designated approver. Share any available customer feedback and campaign data. You do not need to write a creative brief or have direct access to customers: we draft the brief and your approver agrees it. The 7 business days start once payment has cleared, assets are complete, your approver is named and the brief is agreed.

Do you run the ads or guarantee results?

No. We handle the research, angles, copy and static creative. Your team or media buyer handles campaign setup, media buying and optimisation. Results also depend on your offer, landing page and campaign execution. Available results inform future work on the Engine, including its monthly creative review and next-month testing plan.

What counts as an angle or an ad?

An angle is a distinct reason to buy, drawn from the research. An ad is one different graphic with its own hook, message or visual, delivered in one agreed size. Extra sizes are quoted separately and never reduce your count of ads. We agree the mix before production. The Sprint includes 3 angles and 15 different static ads; the Engine includes 4 new angles and up to 30 different static ads across two agreed monthly drops.

How do revisions work?

The Sprint includes one consolidated revision round; the Engine includes one per drop. Your approver sends one collected set of feedback on the agreed direction. New angles, changed briefs or extra rounds require agreed additional scope or a future Engine drop. Corrections to our own mistakes do not use your revision allowance.

What happens if you miss the delivery date?

If we miss the agreed delivery date for reasons within our control, you receive a US$250 service credit or refund, as set out in your signed scope. Deadlines move for late assets, approvals or changed briefs. We correct errors against the approved brief at no extra charge. We do not guarantee CTR, ROAS, CPA or ad winners.

What is a free Creative Read?

A one-page analysis for brands currently running paid ads: a pattern we can confirm in your public ads and two or three hypotheses worth testing, with an invitation to discuss them. We review every request and send a Read where we can do it properly, so it is not an instant or automatic report. We reply within 1 to 2 business days.

How do billing and cancellation work?

The US$1,500 Sprint is a one-time engagement for new clients, paid upfront with no ongoing commitment. Subsequent standalone projects are quoted separately. The US$2,500/month Engine is billed in advance with no minimum multi-month commitment. Cancel before your next billing date to stop renewal; we complete the current paid month’s agreed work. Upgrade within 14 calendar days of Sprint delivery and receive US$250 off your first Creative Engine month: US$2,250 for that month, then US$2,500/month. The credit applies once and cannot be combined with other discounts.

How is this different from a freelancer or agency?

Freelancers, in-house teams and agencies can all do research and strategy. With Kalvano you get it in one defined package: customer and competitor research, angles, copy and finished static ads, at a fixed price with a set delivery date. Your team keeps running the campaigns.

Do you create video ads too?

Our standard packages focus on static paid-social creative. Motion, video editing and repurposing supplied UGC or product footage can be scoped and priced separately. Creator sourcing and shoots are not included.
