01 / The brand

Meet LaloCourse.

LaloCourse is an illustrative online learning platform. This project explores how to make a course feel relevant to someone deciding whether it is worth their time.

Customer premiseMessagingAd design
02 / The challenge

Give someone a reason to make time.

  1. 01Connect a course to a career goal the customer already values.
  2. 02Make the path from learning to practical use easier to picture.
  3. 03Explore different motivations without losing the central message.
03 / The insight

Access to more lessons is an offer. A reason to change your future is a motivation.

The premise we explored: a promotion or career switch can be a stronger reason to learn than access to a large course library.

04 / The creative direction

Turning the premise into an ad.

The central message

From first lesson to career move.

  1. 01

    Lead with the next step.

    Career switching and progression make the learning relevant to a personal ambition.

  2. 02

    Make the path visible.

    Show how a structured learning journey could support that next step.

  3. 03

    Build different ways into the story.

    Explore the goal through a direct headline, a product view and an outcome-led comparison.

05 / What a team could test

Compare the ambition behind the click.

A team could test career progression against career switching, then explore the strongest motivation through different headlines and product demonstrations. The learning-to-career connection stays consistent.
