01 / The brand

Meet Lingo34x.

Lingo34x is an illustrative language-learning app. This project explores how to give someone a reason to subscribe beyond completing another lesson.

Customer premiseMessagingAd design
02 / The challenge

Give progress a real-world meaning.

  1. 01Make the benefit tangible before asking someone to subscribe.
  2. 02Connect the lessons to situations a learner wants to handle.
  3. 03Build several hooks around a consistent, practical promise.
03 / The insight

Completing a lesson can feel productive. Ordering for yourself can make that progress feel real.

The premise we explored: a learner may care more about handling a real conversation than maintaining a streak.

04 / The creative direction

Turning the premise into an ad.

The central message

Order the coffee.

  1. 01

    Start with a familiar moment.

    Coffee orders and reading menus give the customer something specific to picture.

  2. 02

    Connect the moment to the product.

    Show how a lesson could prepare someone for the conversation they want to have.

  3. 03

    Explore the message in different forms.

    Carry the practical benefit through a lifestyle ad, a product demonstration and a comparison.

05 / What a team could test

Test the moment, not just the layout.

Coffee orders, menus and everyday conversations offer different entry points into the same promise. A team could compare which situation earns interest, then refine its hook and execution.
