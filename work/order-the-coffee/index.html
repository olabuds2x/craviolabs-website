Lingo34x

Language-learning app

Illustrative brand and creative. The insight is a working hypothesis, not a research finding or measured campaign result.

The brand & problem
Lingo34x is an illustrative language-learning app. The challenge: give someone a reason to subscribe beyond completing another lesson.
The insight
A learner may care more about handling a real conversation than keeping a streak. A familiar moment can make progress feel useful.
The creative angle

Order the coffee.

The creative system

One central message, explored through everyday situations, a product demonstration, an illustrative testimonial layout and a message comparison.

Six creative examples, including a motion opening frame and a message comparison. Shown as still images.

Why this could work

A specific moment makes the benefit easy to picture and remember. Showing how a lesson connects to that moment gives the customer a practical reason to start.
