01The brand & problem
FocusAI is an illustrative AI meeting assistant. The challenge: make its value clear to teams comparing similar productivity tools.
02The insight
The pain may not be missing notes. It may be people leaving the same meeting with different versions of what was agreed.
03The creative angle

Everyone leaves with the same notes.

The creative system

One central message, explored through shared notes, conflicting recollections, product demonstrations and a message comparison.

Six creative examples, including a motion opening frame and a message comparison. Shown as still images.

Why this could work

Showing the shared record makes an abstract promise concrete. Connecting it to decisions and next steps gives a team a useful reason to consider the product. A performance team can test alignment, accountability and post-meeting confusion as different hooks while keeping the shared-notes promise consistent.
