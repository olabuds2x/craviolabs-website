01 / The brand

Meet FocusAI.

FocusAI is an illustrative AI meeting assistant. This project explores how to make its value clear to teams comparing similar productivity tools.

Customer premiseMessagingAd design
02 / The challenge

Make the value bigger than note-taking.

  1. 01Give a team a concrete reason to consider another productivity tool.
  2. 02Connect the product to decisions, ownership and next steps.
  3. 03Make the benefit visible in the ad itself.
03 / The insight

The meeting ends. The different versions of what was agreed begin.

The premise we explored: the pain may be people remembering decisions differently, even when someone took notes.

04 / The creative direction

Turning the premise into an ad.

The central message

Everyone leaves with the same notes.

  1. 01

    Put the disagreement in view.

    Contrast different recollections of the same meeting with one shared record.

  2. 02

    Show the product doing the job.

    Use notes, decisions and action items to make an abstract promise visible.

  3. 03

    Keep the promise consistent.

    Explore alignment, accountability and post-meeting confusion through different hooks.

05 / What a team could test

Find the friction that matters most.

A team could test conflicting recollections against missed actions and unclear ownership. Each hook leads back to the same benefit, making the message easier to compare across executions.
